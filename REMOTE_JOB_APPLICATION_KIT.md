### Q2: "What is your backup plan if your internet or power goes out?"
> **Answer:**  
> *"I maintain an enterprise-grade redundancy setup: a primary 200 Mbps Fiber line connected to an Uninterruptible Power Supply (UPS) battery backup, plus a secondary 5G wireless mobile hotspot that kicks in automatically if the fiber ever drops. My computer drive is BitLocker-encrypted and I use 1Password/Bitwarden for masked credential access."*

### Q3: "How do you de-escalate an upset or angry customer?"
> **Answer:**  
> *"First, I never take it personally and I never interrupt. I practice active listening and acknowledge their frustration immediately with clear empathy. Second, rather than giving a canned robotic excuse, I immediately investigate our internal order or tracking database to identify the actual breakdown. Third, I offer clear, actionable next steps and follow through until full resolution, then document the scenario into a company SOP so it doesn't happen again."*

### Q4: "How do we know what you're working on without micromanaging you?"
> **Answer:**  
> *"I operate on strict async accountability. At the end of every shift, I post a concise bulleted recap in Slack or Discord and record a quick 2-minute Loom screen video covering: 1) What was completed today, 2) Key metrics hit, and 3) Priorities and automations lined up for tomorrow. All code and documents are pushed directly to GitHub or your knowledge base."*
